# Creative plan

Concept: Everyday, beautifully considered. A cinematic journey from water to flavour, ending in the people and places behind CWAY.

Art direction: official CWAY logo, royal blue and red accents, cool off-white, tightly tracked oversized typography. Full-bleed supplied footage, restrained editorial labels, rounded navigation and generous spacing. No generated packaging or imagery.

Story: CWAY for Every Family → Water, wherever life goes → A little more flavour → Yoghurt, your way → A chocolate kind of moment → Meet Maca Plus → Find your everyday favourite → Four countries. One family. → Care beyond the bottle.

Motion: one sticky cinematic stage per family, scroll-scrubbed supplied frames, 0.12 lerp, six sequential milk flavours with sampled background colours, fade-in/out text beats, subtle text parallax, final ten-frame crossfade when a matching key visual exists. Reduced motion uses static imagery and normal document flow. Navigation and filters remain immediate.

Asset inventory: 12 ZIP archives, 120 JPG frames each, 1440 source frames total. No key visuals or application files supplied. Archives normalized and moved to source/zips; unserved source frames with manifests in source/frames. Missing key visuals hold last frame; reduced-motion falls back to poster. Official images downloaded unchanged for static cards.

Research: local HTML/text snapshots in source/research. Official product pages are authoritative for sizes. Nutri-Yo 500ml / 125ml overrides 400ml filename. Banana verified in Nutri-Milk gallery. No health or certification claims used.
